Dijkstra's Algorithm

Dijkstra's algorithm finds the shortest path from a source vertex to every other vertex in a weighted graph with non-negative edge weights. TravelGraph uses it to compute the shortest route between cities in the academic city graph.

What is Dijkstra?

It's a greedy shortest-path algorithm. Starting from the source city, it repeatedly visits the closest unvisited city and updates the shortest known distance to its neighbours, until every reachable city has been finalized.

How It Works

  1. Start from source, set its distance to 0.
  2. Put the source into a min-priority queue.
  3. Pop the nearest unvisited node.
  4. Check its neighbours.
  5. Relax edges — update shorter distances.
  6. Push updated neighbours back into the queue.
  7. Repeat until destination is finalized.
  8. Reconstruct the shortest path via predecessor pointers.
